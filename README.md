## `$ ls proof-points`

### [BhayanakCast](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/BhayanakCast)

Live screen sharing for small groups at [cast.bhayanak.net](https://cast.bhayanak.net): up to 3 simultaneous shares per room, 1440p60 browser-to-browser WebRTC, Discord sign-in, knock-to-enter private rooms, and 30-day recaps.

`TypeScript` · `TanStack Start` · `WebRTC` · `PostgreSQL` · `Drizzle` · `Playwright`

### [CardsAgainstBhayanak](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/CardsAgainstBhayanak)

Jackbox-style card game at [cards.bhayanak.net](https://cards.bhayanak.net): 70+ packs, 5,000+ cards, room codes with no accounts, all eight official house rules, and 30-second reconnects that keep your seat and hand.

`TypeScript` · `TanStack Start` · `PostgreSQL` · `Redis` · `Vitest` · `Playwright`

### [BhayanakShare](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/bhayanakshare)

AirDrop-style file sharing for Windows and Linux: nearby discovery, direct encrypted transfers with relay fallback, nothing moves until the receiver accepts, resumable transfers, and fingerprint-verified contacts.

`Rust` · `Tauri` · `Iroh` · `Tokio` · `SQLite` · `TypeScript`

### [BhayanakLegends](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/BhayanakLegends)

League of Legends companion: a local improvement journal, post-game reviews, and a live champ-select companion, backed by findings from my own match-statistics research.

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `TypeScript` · `React` · `Tauri` · `Playwright`

### [BhayanakBot](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/BhayanakBot)

Discord bot for the Bhayanak server, in development since 2018: moderation, music, leveling, giveaways, a Guess Who game on a Postgres message archive, and guarded AI replies scoped to the home server.

`TypeScript` · `Sapphire` · `Discord.js` · `PostgreSQL` · `Drizzle` · `Docker`

**Also in the drawer:** [AntSimulation](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/AntSimulation) (Rust/Bevy stigmergy sim) · [BFitLog](https://github.com/theHimanshuShekhar/BFitLog) (self-hosted gym tracker) · [hshekhar.bhayanak.net](https://hshekhar.bhayanak.net) (Astro terminal site)
